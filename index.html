My story

Boston is my reach school.

As a college counselor for more than 20 years, I know there's no magic secret that unlocks the door to your dream school. It's about fit. Authenticity and commitment go further than ambition.

Truth: I'm not fast, but I am dedicated. Earning a BQ was never in the cards, but running Boston is my dream. As a volunteer coach for Girls on the Run, a program I already love, I jumped at the opportunity to wear a charity bib in support of this incredible non-profit. GOTR emphasizes running your own pace, which is what I'll be doing from Hopkinton to Boylston Street.

Running wasn't always my sport, but it has become an outlet for me to find routine, challenge my perceived limitations, and set goals. It requires consistency, early mornings and a great headlamp. After running 2,026+ miles in 2026, I wanted a bigger goal, and that's the Boston Marathon. My dream school.

I'd be so grateful to have your support as I raise $18,000 to ensure finances never prevent a girl from getting to the starting line herself.

Part of what I do as a college counselor is help young people set goals for themselves. And Girls on the Run in many ways feels like a natural extension of that.
